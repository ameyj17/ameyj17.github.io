Amey Joshi

I’m a robotics researcher at New York University building robots that can physically interact with the real world as skillfully and safely as humans. My work centers on contact-rich manipulation in unstructured settings, where I ground real-time decision-making in high-bandwidth tactile perception — not just vision. I design generalizable learning pipelines that intelligently fuse proprioceptive and exteroceptive feedback, enabling robots to handle diverse objects, adapt to unexpected contact, and recover from disturbances. Ultimately, I’m working toward systems that can master complex, force-sensitive tasks with minimal human supervision — turning physical intuition into learnable behavior.
